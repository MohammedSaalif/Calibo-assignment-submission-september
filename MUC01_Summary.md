# Category Intelligence — Retail Product Performance EDA

**Insight Summary** · Prepared for the Quarterly Supplier Review · Based on transaction data, Jan–Jun 2026 (107,836 transactions, 12 stores, 5 categories)

## Key Findings

**1. Electronics is in a sustained decline.**
Category revenue fell from ₹45.7M in January to ₹29.7M in June — a 35% drop — with the decline consistent month over month rather than a one-off dip. Every product in the Electronics line-up is sliding together, most notably Smart TV and Tablet, the two lowest revenue contributors, pointing to a category-wide problem rather than one weak SKU.

**2. Discounting is eroding margin without buying volume.**
Average revenue per transaction falls from ₹4,686 at 0% discount to ₹3,632 at 16–20% discount — a 22% drop — while average units sold per transaction barely moves (2.57 to 2.60 units) regardless of discount depth. Deeper discounts are not driving more purchases; they are simply cutting price on the same basket size.

**3. Apparel and Grocery deserve credit for different reasons.**
Apparel grew 34% over six months — the only category still climbing — making it the strongest case for increased investment this quarter. Grocery grew the least in absolute terms but was the most stable category, moving less than 4% month to month, making it the most dependable performer to plan shelf space and staffing around.

## Category Recommendation

Back Apparel for increased shelf space and promotional budget this quarter. Challenge the Electronics supplier directly on the 35% category-wide decline before agreeing to renewal terms, with particular scrutiny on Smart TV and Tablet. Push back on any supplier proposing deeper discounting as a condition of shelf space — the data does not support it generating incremental revenue.

## Limitation

This analysis is built on revenue and unit-sales data only — it does not include cost or margin data by product. A category can be declining in revenue but stable or improving on margin, or vice versa, and that distinction can't be made from this dataset alone. Margin data by product should be pulled before finalising any supplier decision.
